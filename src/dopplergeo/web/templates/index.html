Rehydrate a past day

From the adsb.lol open archive (ODbL 1.0, all aircraft, one release per UTC day, published about a day later). The whole day (≈3.5 GB) is streamed and only aircraft inside the box are kept. Archive tracks are thinned (a point every ~20 s in straight flight).
